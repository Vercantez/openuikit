# scene # why it is below its bar and not being climbed (measured, written up)
Tabs-classic-row-52 # Tabs t200 classic textLabel cells are 52 pt (contentSize 1560/30, label [16, 0, 343, 52]); tableview_grouped/plain SceneKit cells that use defaultContentConfiguration() are 53 on the same SE 2x / iOS 26.1. Setting defaultRowHeight to 52 matches Tabs but drops tableview_grouped (blob 94 > 80) and Focus Settings (80.345 → 79.82). No discriminator that fits every sample.
corner_radius # 99.41 vs 99.5: every rounded corner on iOS carries a near-tangent coverage ramp about r/2 pt long along the curve (measured 2026-09-04/05 at radii 2-20, 2x and 3x); no closed-form rule fit every sample. A score-targeted parameter fit (exp shoulder + 0.172 radius bias) reached exactly 99.500 and was rejected as a fudge. Needs a rasteriser-level model (filtered SDF?) derived from the per-pixel samples, not tuned to the score.
