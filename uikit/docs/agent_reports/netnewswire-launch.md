## 4. Walls, in the order they will be met

1. **macOS-branch leakage** (160+ app errors and 7 ObjC). This is ios-target's lane and is not hand-patched here. Precise locations:
   - `RSCore/RSImage.swift` `#if os(macOS)` typealiases;
   - the 18 whole-file `#if os(iOS)` files in `RSCore/UIKit/`;
   - `Shared/Assets.swift`, lines 70/98, 137/139, 166/174;
   - the app's ObjC `@import SafariServices`.
2. **On the iOS triple, RSCore** (35):
   - a bitmap `CGContext` + ImageIO surface, and UIKit re-exporting them as Apple's does (22);
   - `String.boundingRect` gating (8);
   - `@objc optional` data-source requirements (2) and `sendAction(Selector)` (1). This is the ObjC-protocol shape that the simplenote objc-core work opened for classes, not yet for protocols.
3. **The app's own 324 non-leakage errors**, listed above. They need re-measuring on the iOS triple first, because many are cascades.
4. **Launch-time services.** A fail-closed answer, never fabricated success, is required for:
   - `FileManager.ubiquityIdentityToken` (it blocked the real simulator);
   - `UNUserNotificationCenter.requestAuthorization` (the alert on the golden);
   - `BGTaskScheduler`;
   - `WidgetKit`;
   - the first-run `DefaultFeedsImporter` refresh. It must see no network: the `NWPathMonitor` above already reports unsatisfied.
5. **Golden determinism.** Mask the counts and favicons, or capture a network-less oracle, before any score.
6. **Guest.** `build_full.sh` has no NetNewsWire modules. The guest's own `full/foundation/os-module/os.swift` also lacks the `os` additions above: it is a separate module from `uikit/Sources/os`.
